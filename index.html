SCIENTIFIC RIGOR & VALIDATION

WHY VEGETATION LOSS ≠ DEFORESTATION

A drop in single-band NDVI can occur from seasonal leaf drop, drought, storm defoliation, or agricultural harvests. ForestWatch executes a 5-vector multi-signal fusion pipeline to isolate true anthropogenic deforestation with scientific precision.

VECTOR 01 // NDVI
Normalized Difference Vegetation Index
NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)

Measures chlorophyll absorption and photosynthetic vigor. A rapid drop indicates canopy biomass reduction.

Enhanced Vegetation Index
EVI = 2.5 × ((NIR - Red) / (NIR + C₁Red - C₂Blue + L))

Corrects for atmospheric aerosols and high-biomass canopy saturation, validating deep tropical rainforest changes.

Normalized Burn Ratio
NBR = (NIR - SWIR) / (NIR + SWIR)

Distinguishes mechanical deforestation from wildfire burn scars. Post-fire SWIR reflectivity spikes; mechanical clearing retains soil signature.
